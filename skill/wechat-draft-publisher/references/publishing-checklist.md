# Publishing Checklist

Use this checklist before creating a WeChat draft.

1. Title is specific and useful, ideally tied to a reader pain point.
2. Intro is short; avoid large self-introduction blocks.
3. Do not expose unprotected project names, book titles, trademarks, or method names.
4. Emphasis is consistent; prefer one accent color over random highlights.
5. Images are readable on mobile and do not contain tiny text.
6. CTA uses a short reply keyword.
7. Cover image exists and matches the article topic.
8. WeChat credentials are loaded from `.env` or environment variables, never committed.
